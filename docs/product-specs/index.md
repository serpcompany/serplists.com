# Product Specs

What the product does today, from the user's point of view. Product principles and
vocabulary are in [PRODUCT_SENSE.md](../PRODUCT_SENSE.md). `pnpm run docs:check`
fails if a spec is missing from this list.

- [Features](features.md): auth, dashboard navigation, Templates, Runs, sharing,
  Organizations, and public discovery as they behave today.
- [Pricing and entitlements](pricing-and-entitlements.md): Free, Pro, and paid
  Organization capabilities, limits, upgrade triggers, and the enforcement contract.
- [Portable templates](portable-templates.md): the import/export and authoring
  format, with copy-paste examples.
